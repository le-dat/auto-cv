# CV Writing Style Guide

Guidelines for writing ATS-optimized CVs that pass applicant tracking systems while remaining human-readable.

## Format Guidelines

- Use standard section headers (Experience, Education, Skills)
- Avoid tables, graphics, or complex formatting
- Use standard bullet points (not special characters)
- Keep to 1-2 pages for most positions

## Content Guidelines

- Include specific metrics and achievements where possible
- Use consistent date formats (YYYY-MM or Month YYYY)
- Focus on recent and relevant experience
- Tailor keywords to match the job description naturally

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Fancy fonts or creative layouts
- Headers/footers that ATS can't read
- Keywords stuffed unnaturally
- Gaps in employment history unexplained
- Contact info hidden in graphics
